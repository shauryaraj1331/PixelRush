About Space Survivor

Space Survivor is an arcade spaceship survival action game. Pilot your scout vessel through hostile cosmic sectors while evading relentless waves of alien ships. Your main blaster turret automatically targets and fires at approaching threats, allowing you to focus on tactical positioning and dodging incoming enemy plasma.

Key Combat Protocols & Intel:

  • Deploy EMP Shockwave: Press Space when cornered or targeted by heavy bullet spreads to disintegrate enemy projectiles and stun nearby hostiles. EMP recharges on a timed cooldown.
  • Harvest XP Energy Cores: Vaporized enemy vessels drop glowing XP cores. Collect them to level up your reactor and select System Upgrades (Multi-shot lasers, overcharged plasma, defensive energy aegis, enhanced thrusters, and magnet fields).
  • Hostile Ship Intel: Fast Scout Interceptors close distance directly, Armored Dreadnoughts absorb heavy damage, Sniper Frigates fire high-velocity railgun bolts from a distance, and Swarm Stingers attack in aggressive packs.
  • Maintain Evasion Corridors: Avoid pinning your vessel against the boundaries of the playfield to preserve 360-degree escape routes.
